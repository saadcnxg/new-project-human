Pituitary Gland

The master gland — a pea-sized organ controlling the entire endocrine system.

Overview

The pituitary gland hangs beneath the hypothalamus at the base of the brain. It releases hormones that control growth, reproduction, and other glands such as the thyroid and adrenals.

~0.5 gWeight

PeaSize

Main Functions

Growth: Growth hormone stimulates tissue growth. Reproduction: FSH and LH regulate gonads. Thyroid: TSH controls thyroid hormone release. Adrenals: ACTH triggers cortisol. Water balance: ADH retains water. Prolactin: Stimulates milk production.

Key Parts

Anterior pituitary (adenohypophysis) and posterior pituitary (neurohypophysis).

Common Issues

Pituitary tumors, gigantism, dwarfism, and diabetes insipidus. Early detection is important for hormone regulation.
