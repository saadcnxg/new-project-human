Esophagus

A muscular tube that pushes swallowed food to the stomach.

Overview

The esophagus is a ~25 cm muscular tube connecting the throat to the stomach. Rhythmic contractions (peristalsis) move food down, and sphincters prevent reflux.

~25 cmLength
~2 sTransit time
2Sphincters
~4Layers

Main Functions

Transport: Moves food via peristalsis. Protection: Mucus shields from acid. Prevention of reflux: Lower sphincter keeps acid in the stomach. Coordination: Works with swallowing reflex.

Key Parts

Mucosa, submucosa, muscularis, adventitia, upper and lower esophageal sphincters.

Common Issues

GERD, esophagitis, achalasia, and esophageal cancer. Eat slowly, avoid large meals, and limit alcohol.
