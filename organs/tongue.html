Tongue

A muscular organ for tasting, chewing, swallowing, and speaking.

Overview

The tongue is a muscular organ in the mouth covered in taste buds. It manipulates food during chewing and swallowing, and is vital for speech articulation.

Main Functions

Taste: Sweet, sour, salty, bitter, umami. Chewing: Moves food for grinding. Swallowing: Pushes food toward the esophagus. Speech: Shapes sounds. Cleaning: Removes food debris from teeth.

Key Parts

Papillae, taste buds, frenulum, and intrinsic and extrinsic muscles.

Common Issues

Oral thrush, tongue ulcers, and taste disorders. Good oral hygiene and hydration maintain tongue health.
