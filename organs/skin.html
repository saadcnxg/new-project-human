Skin

The body's largest organ — a waterproof, self-repairing shield.

Overview

Skin covers the entire body and is the largest organ. It consists of three layers — epidermis, dermis, and hypodermis — and performs many protective and sensory functions.

Main Functions

Protection: Barrier against pathogens, UV, and injury. Temperature: Sweating and vasodilation cool the body. Sensation: Touch, pressure, pain, temperature. Vitamin D: Synthesizes it from sunlight. Excretion: Sweat removes waste.

Key Parts

Epidermis, dermis, hypodermis, hair follicles, sebaceous glands, and sweat glands.

Common Issues

Acne, eczema, psoriasis, burns, and skin cancer. Sun protection and moisturizing maintain skin health.
