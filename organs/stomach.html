Stomach

A muscular sac that churns food and breaks it down with powerful acids.

Overview

The stomach is a J-shaped muscular organ that receives food from the esophagus. It mixes food with gastric juices, initiating protein digestion, and passes the resulting chyme to the small intestine.

~1 LEmpty volume
~4 LFull volume
pH 1.5-3.5Acidity
2-4 hEmptying time

Main Functions

Storage: Holds food between meals. Mechanical digestion: Churns food into chyme. Chemical digestion: HCl and pepsin break down proteins. Protection: Acid kills many pathogens. Absorption: Small amounts of water and alcohol.

Key Parts

Cardia, fundus, body, pylorus, rugae, gastric glands, and pyloric sphincter.

Common Issues

Gastritis, ulcers, acid reflux, and stomach cancer. Eat balanced meals, limit spicy and acidic foods, and avoid NSAIDs overuse.
