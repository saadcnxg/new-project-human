Gallbladder

A small pear-shaped sac that stores bile for fat digestion.

Overview

The gallbladder sits under the liver. It stores and concentrates bile produced by the liver and releases it into the small intestine when fatty food arrives.

~8 cmLength
~50 mLCapacity
~5xBile concentration
1Sac

Main Functions

Bile storage: Holds bile between meals. Concentration: Removes water to strengthen bile. Release: Contracts when fat is present to release bile. Fat emulsification: Aids lipid digestion.

Key Parts

Fundus, body, neck, cystic duct, and common bile duct.

Common Issues

Gallstones, cholecystitis, and gallbladder polyps. Low-fat diets and gradual weight loss reduce risk.
