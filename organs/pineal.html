Pineal Gland

A tiny pine-cone-shaped gland that sets your body's internal clock.

Overview

The pineal gland sits deep in the brain's center. It produces melatonin in response to darkness, regulating sleep-wake cycles and seasonal biological rhythms.

~0.1 gWeight
MelatoninMain hormone
~24 hCycle regulated
1Gland

Main Functions

Circadian rhythm: Synchronizes the body clock with day and night. Melatonin production: Peaks at night to promote sleep. Reproductive timing: Influences seasonal reproduction in some species.

Key Parts

Pinealocytes, glial cells, and a rich blood supply.

Common Issues

Pineal tumors, calcification, and melatonin imbalance. Good sleep hygiene and limiting blue light at night support pineal health.
