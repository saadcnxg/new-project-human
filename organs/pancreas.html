Pancreas

A dual-purpose gland that produces digestive enzymes and blood sugar hormones.

Overview

The pancreas sits behind the stomach. It has an exocrine role (secreting digestive enzymes into the small intestine) and an endocrine role (producing insulin and glucagon in the islets of Langerhans).

Main Functions

Digestion: Secretes enzymes for proteins, fats, and carbohydrates. Blood sugar: Insulin lowers glucose; glucagon raises it. Bicarbonate: Neutralizes stomach acid entering the intestine.

Key Parts

Head, body, tail, pancreatic duct, acini, and islets of Langerhans.

Common Issues

Pancreatitis, pancreatic cancer, diabetes, and cystic fibrosis-related damage. Limit alcohol, avoid smoking, and maintain healthy weight.
