Eyes

Paired sensory organs that convert light into vision.

Overview

The eyes are complex organs that focus light onto the retina, where photoreceptor cells convert it into electrical signals sent to the brain for interpretation.

~120MRods
~6MCones
~576Megapixels equivalent
~2Eyes

Main Functions

Vision: Detects light and images. Focus: Lens adjusts for distance. Color: Cones detect wavelengths. Night vision: Rods sense low light. Depth perception: Binocular vision enables 3D sight.

Key Parts

Cornea, iris, pupil, lens, retina, optic nerve, and vitreous humor.

Common Issues

Myopia, hyperopia, astigmatism, cataracts, glaucoma, and macular degeneration. Regular eye exams and UV protection are important.
