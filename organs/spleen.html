Spleen

A blood-filtering organ that supports immunity and recycles red blood cells.

Overview

The spleen is the largest lymphatic organ, located in the upper left abdomen. It filters blood, removes old red blood cells, and helps fight certain infections.

Main Functions

Blood filtration: Removes old RBCs and pathogens. Immune support: Produces lymphocytes and macrophages. Blood storage: Holds a reserve of platelets and RBCs. Recycling: Breaks down hemoglobin into bilirubin.

Key Parts

Red pulp (filters blood), white pulp (immune), capsule, and splenic artery and vein.

Common Issues

Splenomegaly, splenic rupture, and hypersplenism. People without a spleen need vaccinations and infection precautions.
