Adrenal Glands

Two small glands atop the kidneys that power your fight-or-flight response.

Overview

The adrenal glands are triangular glands on top of each kidney. They have an outer cortex (producing cortisol, aldosterone, androgens) and an inner medulla (producing adrenaline and noradrenaline).

Main Functions

Stress response: Adrenaline surges raise heart rate and energy. Cortisol: Regulates metabolism and immune response. Aldosterone: Controls salt and water balance. Androgens: Contribute to sex hormone production.

Key Parts

Cortex (zona glomerulosa, fasciculata, reticularis) and medulla.

Common Issues

Addison's disease, Cushing's syndrome, and pheochromocytoma. Managing stress and regular checkups help.
