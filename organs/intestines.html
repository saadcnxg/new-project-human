Intestines

A coiled 7-meter tube where most digestion and nutrient absorption happens.

Overview

The intestines are divided into the small intestine (duodenum, jejunum, ileum) where absorption occurs, and the large intestine (colon) which absorbs water and forms waste.

~7 mSmall intestine
~1.5 mLarge intestine
~250 m2Absorption area
~100TMicrobes in gut

Main Functions

Absorption: Nutrients enter the bloodstream through villi. Water reabsorption: The colon reclaims water. Microbiome: Gut bacteria support digestion and immunity. Waste formation: Undigested material is compacted into stool.

Key Parts

Duodenum, jejunum, ileum, cecum, colon (ascending, transverse, descending, sigmoid), and rectum.

Common Issues

IBS, Crohn's disease, ulcerative colitis, celiac disease, and colon cancer. Fiber, hydration, and regular screenings are important after age 45.
