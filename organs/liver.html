Liver

The body's chemical factory — over 500 functions, from detox to digestion.

Overview

The liver is the largest internal organ, located in the upper right abdomen. It processes nutrients, detoxifies blood, produces bile, and stores vitamins and minerals.

Main Functions

Detoxification: Breaks down toxins, drugs, and alcohol. Metabolism: Regulates glucose, fats, and proteins. Bile production: Aids fat digestion. Storage: Holds glycogen, vitamins A, D, B12, and iron. Immune: Contains Kupffer cells that destroy pathogens.

Key Parts

Right and left lobes, hepatic artery, portal vein, bile ducts, and gallbladder (attached).

Common Issues

Fatty liver, hepatitis, cirrhosis, and liver cancer. Limit alcohol, maintain healthy weight, and get vaccinated for hepatitis.
