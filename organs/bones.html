Bones

Living tissue — 206 bones that support, protect, and produce blood.

Overview

Bones are dynamic living organs made of collagen, minerals, and cells. They provide the body's framework, protect organs, store minerals, and house bone marrow for blood formation.

206Adult bones
~15%Body weight
300Bones at birth
~2.5MRBCs made/sec

Main Functions

Support: Frame and posture. Protection: Shield organs. Movement: Lever system with muscles. Mineral storage: Calcium and phosphorus. Blood production: Bone marrow creates RBCs and immune cells.

Key Parts

Compact bone, spongy bone, periosteum, medullary cavity, and bone marrow.

Common Issues

Osteoporosis, fractures, and bone cancer. Weight-bearing exercise, calcium, and vitamin D keep bones strong.
