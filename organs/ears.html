Ears

Organs of hearing and balance, translating sound waves into signals.

Overview

The ear has three parts — outer, middle, and inner. It converts sound waves into electrical signals for the brain and helps control balance via the vestibular system.

~20 HzLowest pitch

Main Functions

Hearing: Detects and interprets sound. Balance: Semicircular canals detect movement. Localization: Determines sound direction. Amplification: Ossicles magnify vibrations.

Key Parts

Pinna, ear canal, eardrum, ossicles (malleus, incus, stapes), cochlea, and vestibular apparatus.

Common Issues

Hearing loss, tinnitus, otitis media, and vertigo. Avoid loud noise and protect ears from infections.
