Thyroid

A butterfly-shaped gland that sets the pace of your metabolism.

Overview

The thyroid gland sits at the base of the neck. It produces thyroid hormones (T3 and T4) that regulate metabolism, growth, and body temperature, and calcitonin which helps control calcium.

Main Functions

Metabolism: Controls how fast cells burn energy. Growth: Supports development in children. Heart rate: Influences cardiovascular function. Calcium: Calcitonin lowers blood calcium. Temperature: Regulates heat production.

Key Parts

Right and left lobes, isthmus, follicular cells, and parafollicular C cells.

Common Issues

Hypothyroidism, hyperthyroidism, goiter, thyroid nodules, and thyroid cancer. Iodine intake and regular checkups help detect problems early.
