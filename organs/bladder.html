Bladder

A stretchy muscular sac that stores urine until it's time to release it.

Overview

The urinary bladder is a hollow muscular organ in the pelvis. It stores urine produced by the kidneys and empties through the urethra when full.

Main Functions

Storage: Holds urine until convenient to void. Emptying: Detrusor muscle contracts to expel urine. Sphincter control: Internal and external sphincters regulate flow.

Key Parts

Detrusor muscle, trigone, internal urethral sphincter, external urethral sphincter, and urethra.

Common Issues

Urinary tract infections, incontinence, overactive bladder, and bladder stones. Hydration and timely voiding support bladder health.
