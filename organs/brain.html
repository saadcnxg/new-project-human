Brain

The command center — 86 billion neurons controlling thought, movement, and emotion.

Overview

The brain is the central organ of the nervous system, protected by the skull and meninges. It processes sensory information, controls movement, regulates body functions, and enables consciousness, memory, and emotion.

Main Functions

Cognition: Thinking, reasoning, and memory. Motor control: Directs voluntary movement. Sensation: Interprets sight, sound, touch, taste, and smell. Homeostasis: Regulates breathing, heart rate, and temperature. Emotion: Processes feelings and behavior.

Key Parts

Cerebrum (frontal, parietal, temporal, occipital lobes), cerebellum, brainstem (midbrain, pons, medulla), thalamus, and hypothalamus.

Common Issues

Stroke, Alzheimer's disease, Parkinson's disease, epilepsy, traumatic brain injury, and migraines. Sleep, exercise, and mental stimulation support brain health.
