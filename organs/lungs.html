Lungs

Spongy paired organs that exchange oxygen and carbon dioxide with the blood.

Overview

The lungs are two cone-shaped organs in the chest cavity. They contain millions of alveoli where gas exchange occurs. The right lung has three lobes; the left has two to make room for the heart.

Main Functions

Gas exchange: Oxygen enters blood; CO2 is expelled. pH balance: Regulates blood acidity via CO2 levels. Filtration: Traps particles and pathogens. Vocalization: Airflow through the larynx produces voice.

Key Parts

Trachea, bronchi, bronchioles, alveoli, pleura, and diaphragm.

Common Issues

Asthma, COPD, pneumonia, bronchitis, tuberculosis, and lung cancer. Avoid smoking and pollution, and exercise regularly.
