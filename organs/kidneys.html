Kidneys

Bean-shaped filters that clean the blood and balance the body's water and salts.

Overview

Two kidneys sit behind the abdomen. Each contains about a million nephrons that filter blood, remove waste, and produce urine while maintaining electrolyte and acid-base balance.

Main Functions

Filtration: Removes urea, creatinine, and toxins. Reabsorption: Returns water, glucose, and amino acids to blood. Blood pressure: Regulates via renin. Red blood cells: Produces erythropoietin. Vitamin D: Activates it for bone health.

Key Parts

Renal cortex, medulla, renal pelvis, nephrons, glomeruli, and ureters.

Common Issues

Kidney stones, UTIs, chronic kidney disease, and kidney failure. Stay hydrated, limit salt, and control blood pressure and blood sugar.
