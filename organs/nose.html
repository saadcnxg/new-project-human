Nose

The gateway to breathing and the seat of the sense of smell.

Overview

The nose filters, warms, and humidifies inhaled air. It also contains olfactory receptors for the sense of smell and helps shape the voice.

Main Functions

Breathing: Primary airway. Filtration: Traps particles and pathogens. Humidification: Adds moisture and warmth. Smell: Detects airborne molecules. Resonance: Affects voice quality.

Key Parts

Nostrils, nasal cavity, septum, turbinates, and olfactory epithelium.

Common Issues

Allergies, sinusitis, deviated septum, and anosmia (loss of smell). Nasal hygiene and avoiding irritants help.
