Heart

A muscular pump the size of your fist that beats ~100,000 times a day.

Overview

The heart is a four-chambered muscular organ located in the chest behind the sternum. It pumps blood through the pulmonary and systemic circulations, delivering oxygen and nutrients to every cell while removing waste.

Main Functions

Pumping: Moves blood through the body. Oxygenation: Sends deoxygenated blood to the lungs. Circulation: Distributes nutrients and hormones. Pressure: Maintains blood pressure to perfuse organs.

Key Parts

Right and left atria, right and left ventricles, valves (mitral, tricuspid, aortic, pulmonary), coronary arteries, and the cardiac conduction system (SA node, AV node).

Common Issues

Coronary artery disease, heart attack, arrhythmia, heart failure, and valve disorders. Exercise, balanced diet, and avoiding smoking protect heart health.
