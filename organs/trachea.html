Trachea

The windpipe — a cartilage-ringed tube carrying air to the lungs.

Overview

The trachea is a ~10-12 cm tube descending from the larynx. It splits into two bronchi that lead to the lungs. C-shaped cartilage rings keep it open.

~10 cmLength
~16-20Cartilage rings
2Bronchi branches
~2 cmDiameter

Main Functions

Airway: Conducts air to and from the lungs. Filtration: Cilia and mucus trap particles. Warming and humidifying: Prepares air for the lungs. Protection: Cough reflex clears irritants.

Key Parts

Cartilage rings, ciliated epithelium, mucus glands, and carina (the split point).

Common Issues

Tracheitis, tracheal stenosis, and obstruction by foreign objects. Avoid smoking and get prompt care for breathing issues.
