Appendix

A small pouch attached to the colon, with a possible role in immunity.

Overview

The appendix is a worm-like tube attached to the cecum. Once thought vestigial, it may house gut bacteria reserves and lymphatic tissue that supports immunity.

~9 cmLength
~1Organ
LymphoidTissue rich
~7%Lifetime risk of appendicitis

Main Functions

Bacterial reservoir: May restore gut flora after illness. Immune tissue: Contains lymphoid follicles. Vestigial: Has no essential digestive role.

Key Parts

Base, body, tip, and mesoappendix.

Common Issues

Appendicitis (requires urgent surgery), and appendiceal tumors. Prompt medical attention for right-lower abdominal pain is essential.
