Heart Disease

A broad term for conditions affecting the heart and blood vessels — the leading cause of death worldwide.

Overview

Heart disease includes coronary artery disease, heart failure, arrhythmias, and valve problems. The most common cause is atherosclerosis — plaque buildup narrowing arteries.

~18MDeaths/year
#1Global cause
4Major types
~80%Preventable

Common Symptoms

Chest pain, shortness of breath, palpitations, fatigue, and swelling in the legs. Heart attack signs include crushing chest pain, arm pain, sweating, and nausea.

Causes & Risk Factors

High blood pressure, high cholesterol, smoking, diabetes, obesity, inactivity, unhealthy diet, and family history.

Prevention & Management

Eat a heart-healthy diet (Mediterranean-style), exercise regularly, quit smoking, control blood pressure and cholesterol, and take prescribed medications. Call emergency services for heart attack symptoms.
