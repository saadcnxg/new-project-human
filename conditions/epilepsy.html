Epilepsy

A neurological disorder marked by recurrent, unprovoked seizures.

Overview

Epilepsy involves abnormal electrical activity in the brain. Seizures can be focal (one area) or generalized (whole brain). Many cases are well-controlled with medication.

SeizuresMain symptom

Common Symptoms

Convulsions, loss of consciousness, staring spells, confusion, unusual sensations, and muscle stiffness or jerking.

Causes & Risk Factors

Head trauma, stroke, brain tumors, genetic factors, and infections of the brain. In many cases, the cause is unknown.

Prevention & Management

Take prescribed anticonvulsant medications, avoid triggers (sleep deprivation, alcohol), and get regular medical care. Surgery or dietary therapy (ketogenic) may help refractory cases.
