Stroke

A medical emergency where blood flow to part of the brain is interrupted.

Overview

Strokes occur when a blood vessel in the brain is blocked (ischemic, ~85%) or bursts (hemorrhagic). Brain cells die within minutes, so immediate care is critical.

~15MCases/year
~5MDeaths/year
FASTEmergency signs
4.5 hClot-buster window

Common Symptoms

Use FAST: Face drooping, Arm weakness, Speech difficulty, Time to call emergency. Other signs: sudden severe headache, vision loss, or balance problems.

Causes & Risk Factors

Hypertension, smoking, diabetes, high cholesterol, atrial fibrillation, obesity, and family history.

Prevention & Management

Control blood pressure, exercise, avoid smoking, and take prescribed anticoagulants. After a stroke, rehabilitation (physical, speech, occupational therapy) improves recovery.
