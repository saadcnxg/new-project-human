Hepatitis

Inflammation of the liver, most often caused by viral infection.

Overview

Hepatitis A, B, and C are the most common types. B and C can become chronic and lead to cirrhosis or liver cancer. A is usually acute and self-limiting.

Common Symptoms

Fatigue, jaundice (yellow skin and eyes), dark urine, abdominal pain, nausea, and loss of appetite. Many cases are silent for years.

Causes & Risk Factors

Hep A: contaminated food/water. Hep B/C: blood, sexual contact, needle sharing, and mother-to-child transmission.

Prevention & Management

Get vaccinated for A and B, practice safe sex, avoid needle sharing, and use clean water and food. Hep C is curable with antiviral therapy. Avoid alcohol if infected.
