Parkinson's Disease

A progressive nervous system disorder affecting movement and coordination.

Overview

Parkinson's is caused by the loss of dopamine-producing neurons in the substantia nigra of the brain. It leads to tremor, rigidity, and slow movement.

Common Symptoms

Tremor at rest, slow movement (bradykinesia), muscle rigidity, stooped posture, balance problems, and eventually cognitive changes.

Causes & Risk Factors

Age, genetics, environmental toxins, and head injuries. Most cases are idiopathic.

Prevention & Management

No known prevention. Management includes levodopa-carbidopa, physical and speech therapy, deep brain stimulation, and exercise.
