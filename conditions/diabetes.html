Diabetes

A chronic condition where blood sugar stays too high because the body can't use insulin properly.

Overview

Diabetes affects how the body turns food into energy. In type 1, the body produces little or no insulin; in type 2, cells resist insulin. Over time, high blood sugar damages nerves, kidneys, eyes, and blood vessels.

Common Symptoms

Excessive thirst, frequent urination, fatigue, blurred vision, slow-healing wounds, and unexplained weight loss (type 1).

Causes & Risk Factors

Type 1 is autoimmune. Type 2 is linked to genetics, excess weight, inactivity, and poor diet. Gestational diabetes occurs during pregnancy.

Prevention & Management

Balanced diet, regular exercise, weight control, blood sugar monitoring, and medication or insulin as prescribed. Regular checkups catch complications early.
