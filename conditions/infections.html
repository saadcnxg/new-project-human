Infections

Diseases caused by pathogens — bacteria, viruses, fungi, or parasites — invading the body.

Overview

Infections range from mild (cold, flu) to life-threatening (sepsis, severe pneumonia). Transmission occurs through air, contact, food, water, or vectors like mosquitoes.

Common Symptoms

Fever, fatigue, body aches, cough, sore throat, diarrhea, and inflammation at the site of infection.

Causes & Risk Factors

Weak immunity, poor hygiene, contaminated food/water, travel, and close contact with infected people. Overuse of antibiotics fuels resistance.

Prevention & Management

Wash hands, get vaccinated, cook food safely, avoid sick contacts, and use antibiotics only as prescribed. Complete all treatment courses.
