Hypertension

Persistently high blood pressure that silently strains the heart, brain, and kidneys.

Overview

Hypertension means blood pressure stays ≥130/80 mmHg over time. It's often called the "silent killer" because it usually has no symptoms until damage occurs.

130/80High threshold
~1.3BAdults globally
2Main types
DASHDiet helps

Common Symptoms

Mostly none. Severe cases may cause headaches, nosebleeds, shortness of breath, or vision changes.

Causes & Risk Factors

High salt intake, obesity, inactivity, smoking, alcohol, stress, age, and genetics. Secondary causes include kidney disease and hormonal disorders.

Prevention & Management

Reduce sodium, exercise 150 min/week, maintain healthy weight, limit alcohol, quit smoking, and take prescribed medications.
