Asthma

A chronic lung condition where airways narrow and swell, making breathing difficult.

Overview

Asthma causes inflammation and tightening of the bronchial tubes, leading to episodes of wheezing, coughing, and breathlessness. It can be triggered by allergens, exercise, cold air, or stress.

Common Symptoms

Wheezing, chest tightness, shortness of breath, and coughing (especially at night or during exercise).

Causes & Risk Factors

Genetics, allergies, air pollution, smoking, respiratory infections, and obesity. Triggers vary by person.

Prevention & Management

Avoid triggers, use reliever and preventer inhalers, follow an asthma action plan, and get flu vaccines. Seek emergency care for severe attacks.
