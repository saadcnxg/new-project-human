Alzheimer's Disease

A progressive brain disorder that slowly destroys memory and thinking skills.

Overview

Alzheimer's is the most common cause of dementia. It involves buildup of amyloid plaques and tau tangles in the brain, leading to neuron death and brain shrinkage.

Common Symptoms

Memory loss, difficulty with words, confusion about time/place, poor judgment, mood changes, and withdrawal from activities.

Causes & Risk Factors

Age, genetics (APOE-e4), family history, head injuries, and cardiovascular risk factors.

Prevention & Management

Stay mentally and socially active, exercise, eat a Mediterranean-style diet, and manage blood pressure and diabetes. Medications may slow symptoms.
