Arthritis

Inflammation of the joints causing pain, stiffness, and reduced movement.

Overview

Arthritis is a group of over 100 conditions affecting joints. Osteoarthritis (wear-and-tear) and rheumatoid arthritis (autoimmune) are the most common. Symptoms worsen with age and can significantly affect quality of life.

Common Symptoms

Joint pain, swelling, stiffness (especially morning stiffness), reduced range of motion, and warmth around affected joints.

Causes & Risk Factors

Age, obesity, joint injury, genetics, and autoimmune factors (for rheumatoid). Overuse of joints also contributes.

Prevention & Management

Maintain healthy weight, do low-impact exercise (swimming, cycling), physical therapy, and use medications (NSAIDs, DMARDs) as prescribed. Heat/cold therapy helps symptoms.
