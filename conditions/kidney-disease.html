Kidney Disease

Gradual loss of kidney function that impairs the body's ability to filter waste.

Overview

Chronic kidney disease (CKD) develops over months or years and often goes unnoticed until advanced. Severe cases require dialysis or a transplant.

Common Symptoms

Swelling in legs and ankles, fatigue, poor appetite, foamy urine, frequent urination (especially at night), and itchy skin.

Causes & Risk Factors

Diabetes, hypertension, glomerulonephritis, polycystic kidney disease, and prolonged use of certain medications.

Prevention & Management

Control blood sugar and blood pressure, avoid excessive salt, stay hydrated, avoid NSAID overuse, and get regular kidney function tests if at risk.
