Anxiety Disorders

Excessive, persistent worry or fear that interferes with daily life.

Overview

Anxiety disorders include generalized anxiety, panic disorder, social anxiety, and phobias. They involve overactivation of the body's stress response even when no real threat exists.

~300MPeople affected

CBTEffective therapy

Common Symptoms

Racing thoughts, restlessness, rapid heartbeat, sweating, trembling, trouble concentrating, and sleep problems.

Causes & Risk Factors

Genetics, brain chemistry, trauma, chronic stress, and personality traits. Caffeine and some medications can worsen symptoms.

Prevention & Management

Cognitive behavioral therapy, relaxation techniques (breathing, meditation), regular exercise, and medications (SSRIs) when prescribed. Limit caffeine and alcohol.
