Thyroid Disorders

Conditions where the thyroid produces too much or too little hormone.

Overview

The two main thyroid disorders are hypothyroidism (underactive) and hyperthyroidism (overactive). Both affect metabolism, energy, and mood. Goiter and nodules are also common.

Common Symptoms

Hypothyroidism: fatigue, weight gain, cold intolerance, dry skin, constipation. Hyperthyroidism: weight loss, heat intolerance, rapid heartbeat, anxiety, tremor.

Causes & Risk Factors

Autoimmune diseases (Hashimoto's, Graves'), iodine deficiency or excess, thyroid surgery, and certain medications. Family history increases risk.

Prevention & Management

Use iodized salt, get regular checkups if at risk, and take prescribed hormones or antithyroid medications. Surgery or radioactive iodine may be needed in some cases.
