Osteoporosis

A condition where bones become weak and brittle, making fractures more likely.

Overview

Osteoporosis silently thins bone tissue and reduces density, particularly in postmenopausal women. It often goes unnoticed until a fracture occurs.

~200MPeople affected

DEXADiagnostic scan
SilentUntil fracture

Common Symptoms

Often asymptomatic. May show as back pain, loss of height, stooped posture, or easy fractures (hips, wrists, spine).

Causes & Risk Factors

Estrogen loss after menopause, aging, low calcium/vitamin D, inactivity, smoking, alcohol, and long-term steroid use.

Prevention & Management

Get adequate calcium (1200 mg/day) and vitamin D, do weight-bearing and resistance exercises, avoid smoking, and consider medications if high risk.
