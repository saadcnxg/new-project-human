Obesity

A chronic condition of excess body fat that increases risk of many other diseases.

Overview

Obesity is defined as a BMI of 30 or higher. It results from an energy imbalance where calorie intake exceeds expenditure, and it affects hormones, metabolism, and inflammation.

Common Symptoms

Excess body fat, shortness of breath, fatigue, joint pain, and increased sweating. It raises risk of diabetes, heart disease, and some cancers.

Causes & Risk Factors

High-calorie diet, inactivity, genetics, certain medications, hormonal disorders, poor sleep, and stress.

Prevention & Management

Balanced diet with portion control, 150+ min of exercise/week, adequate sleep, stress management, and medical support (dietitian, medications, or surgery in severe cases).
