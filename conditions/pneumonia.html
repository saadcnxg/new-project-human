Pneumonia

An infection that inflames the air sacs in one or both lungs, filling them with fluid.

Overview

Pneumonia can be caused by bacteria, viruses, or fungi. The alveoli fill with fluid or pus, making breathing difficult and reducing oxygen exchange.

Common Symptoms

Cough with phlegm, fever, chills, shortness of breath, chest pain when breathing, and fatigue. In older adults, confusion may be the main sign.

Causes & Risk Factors

Bacteria (pneumococcus), viruses (flu, COVID-19), and fungi. Risk increases with age, smoking, chronic lung disease, and weakened immunity.

Prevention & Management

Get vaccinated (pneumococcal, flu), wash hands, don't smoke, and seek prompt treatment with antibiotics (bacterial) or antivirals (viral). Rest and hydrate during recovery.
