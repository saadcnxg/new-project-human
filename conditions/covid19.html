COVID-19

A respiratory illness caused by the SARS-CoV-2 virus, ranging from mild to severe.

Overview

COVID-19 spreads mainly through respiratory droplets. It can affect the lungs, heart, brain, and other organs. Vaccines greatly reduce severe outcomes.

2019First identified
AirborneMain spread
VaccinesAvailable
VariantsEvolve over time

Common Symptoms

Fever, cough, fatigue, loss of taste or smell, sore throat, and shortness of breath. Some develop long COVID with persistent symptoms.

Causes & Risk Factors

SARS-CoV-2 infection. Risk of severe disease is higher in older adults, immunocompromised people, and those with chronic conditions.

Prevention & Management

Stay up to date with vaccines, wash hands, improve ventilation, and isolate when sick. Antivirals may be prescribed for high-risk patients. Seek emergency care for severe breathing difficulty.
