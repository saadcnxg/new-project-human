Tuberculosis

A bacterial infection that mainly attacks the lungs, spread through the air.

Overview

TB is caused by Mycobacterium tuberculosis. It can be latent (dormant) or active. Active TB causes serious lung damage and is a leading infectious killer.

Common Symptoms

Persistent cough (3+ weeks), coughing blood, chest pain, fatigue, weight loss, fever, and night sweats.

Causes & Risk Factors

Close contact with infected people, weakened immunity (HIV, diabetes), overcrowding, and malnutrition.

Prevention & Management

BCG vaccine, good ventilation, masking, and completing the full antibiotic regimen (DOTS) to prevent drug resistance.
