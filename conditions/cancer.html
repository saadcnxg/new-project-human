Cancer

A group of diseases where abnormal cells grow uncontrollably and invade tissues.

Overview

Cancer arises when genetic mutations cause cells to divide without control. It can start almost anywhere in the body and may spread (metastasize) to other organs.

Common Symptoms

Unexplained weight loss, persistent fatigue, unusual lumps, changes in bowel or bladder habits, non-healing sores, and chronic cough or hoarseness.

Causes & Risk Factors

Smoking, alcohol, obesity, poor diet, inactivity, UV exposure, infections (HPV, hepatitis), genetics, and environmental toxins.

Prevention & Management

Don't smoke, limit alcohol, maintain healthy weight, eat fruits and vegetables, exercise, use sun protection, get vaccinated (HPV, hepatitis B), and attend screenings (mammogram, colonoscopy).
