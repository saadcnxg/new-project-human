Allergies

Overreactions of the immune system to harmless substances like pollen, food, or dust.

Overview

Allergies occur when the immune system mistakes a harmless substance (allergen) for a threat and releases histamine, causing symptoms ranging from mild to life-threatening anaphylaxis.

Common Symptoms

Sneezing, runny nose, itchy eyes, skin rash, hives, swelling, wheezing. Anaphylaxis causes throat swelling, low blood pressure, and difficulty breathing.

Causes & Risk Factors

Genetics, hygiene hypothesis, pollution, and early-life exposures. Common allergens: pollen, dust mites, pet dander, foods (nuts, shellfish), insect stings, and medications.

Prevention & Management

Avoid known allergens, use antihistamines and nasal sprays, carry epinephrine if prescribed, and consider allergy shots for long-term relief.
