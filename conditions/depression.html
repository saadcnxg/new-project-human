Depression

A common mood disorder causing persistent sadness, low energy, and loss of interest.

Overview

Depression is more than sadness — it's a medical condition affecting how you think, feel, and function. It involves brain chemistry, genetics, and environmental factors.

~280MPeople affected

Common Symptoms

Persistent sadness, loss of interest, fatigue, sleep changes, appetite changes, difficulty concentrating, and thoughts of self-harm.

Causes & Risk Factors

Genetics, brain chemistry imbalance, trauma, chronic illness, social isolation, and major life stress.

Prevention & Management

Seek professional help (therapy, medication), exercise regularly, maintain social connections, get good sleep, and reach out to support services. Crisis lines are available 24/7.
