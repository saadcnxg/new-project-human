Anemia

A condition where the blood lacks enough healthy red blood cells to carry oxygen.

Overview

Anemia reduces oxygen delivery to tissues. Iron-deficiency is the most common type globally, but other forms include B12-deficiency, hemolytic, and sickle-cell anemia.

Common Symptoms

Fatigue, pale skin, dizziness, cold hands and feet, shortness of breath, and rapid heartbeat.

Causes & Risk Factors

Poor iron intake, blood loss (menstruation, ulcers), pregnancy, chronic disease, B12/folate deficiency, and genetic disorders.

Prevention & Management

Eat iron-rich foods (red meat, lentils, spinach), pair with vitamin C for absorption, take supplements if prescribed, and treat underlying causes.
