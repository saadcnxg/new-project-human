Migraine

A neurological condition causing intense, throbbing headaches often with nausea and light sensitivity.

Overview

Migraines are recurring attacks of moderate-to-severe headache, usually on one side of the head, lasting 4–72 hours. They may be preceded by an aura (visual or sensory disturbance).

Common Symptoms

Throbbing head pain, nausea, vomiting, sensitivity to light and sound, and visual aura (flashing lights, blind spots).

Causes & Risk Factors

Genetics, hormonal changes, stress, poor sleep, certain foods (caffeine, alcohol, aged cheese), and bright lights.

Prevention & Management

Identify triggers, keep a headache diary, maintain regular sleep and meals, and use acute or preventive medications as prescribed. Rest in a dark, quiet room during attacks.
