Respiratory System

Oxygen in, carbon dioxide out — the breath of life.

Overview

The respiratory system moves air in and out of the lungs, where oxygen enters the blood and carbon dioxide is expelled. It includes the nose, pharynx, larynx, trachea, bronchi, and lungs.

Main Functions

Ventilation: Movement of air in and out. Gas exchange: Oxygen into blood, CO2 out. pH regulation: Controls blood acidity. Speech and smell: Airflow enables vocalization and olfaction.

Key Parts

Nose, pharynx, larynx, trachea, bronchi, bronchioles, alveoli, diaphragm, and intercostal muscles.

Common Issues

Asthma, COPD, bronchitis, pneumonia, tuberculosis, and lung cancer. Avoid smoking, exercise regularly, and maintain good air quality.
