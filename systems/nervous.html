Nervous System

The body's control center — processing sensation and directing every action.

Overview

The nervous system comprises the central nervous system (brain and spinal cord) and peripheral nervous system (nerves). It receives sensory input, processes information, and commands responses.

Main Functions

Sensory: Detect touch, temperature, pain, and internal states. Integration: Process and interpret information. Motor: Command muscles and glands. Cognition: Thought, memory, emotion, and consciousness.

Key Parts

Brain (cerebrum, cerebellum, brainstem), spinal cord, 12 cranial nerves, 31 spinal nerve pairs, and autonomic nervous system (sympathetic and parasympathetic).

Common Issues

Headaches, migraines, epilepsy, stroke, Alzheimer's, Parkinson's, and multiple sclerosis. Sleep, exercise, and mental stimulation support nervous health.
