Endocrine System

Glands and hormones that regulate growth, metabolism, mood, and reproduction.

Overview

The endocrine system uses hormones as chemical messengers to regulate long-term processes: growth, metabolism, sexual development, mood, and homeostasis.

Main Functions

Metabolism: Thyroid hormones control energy use. Growth: Growth hormone from pituitary. Stress: Cortisol from adrenals. Reproduction: Sex hormones from gonads. Blood sugar: Insulin and glucagon from pancreas.

Key Parts

Hypothalamus, pituitary, thyroid, parathyroid, adrenals, pancreas, ovaries, and testes.

Common Issues

Diabetes, hypothyroidism, hyperthyroidism, Cushing's, Addison's, and PCOS. Regular checkups help detect hormonal imbalances early.
