Lymphatic System

A network of vessels and nodes that balances fluid and supports immunity.

Overview

The lymphatic system returns excess tissue fluid to the bloodstream and transports white blood cells. It includes lymph vessels, lymph nodes, spleen, thymus, and tonsils.

Main Functions

Fluid balance: Returns tissue fluid to blood. Fat absorption: Lacteals in intestines absorb fats. Immunity: Lymph nodes filter pathogens and activate white blood cells. Waste removal: Clears cellular debris.

Key Parts

Lymph capillaries, vessels, nodes, tonsils, thymus, spleen, and bone marrow.

Common Issues

Lymphedema, lymphoma, tonsillitis, and lymphadenitis. Movement, hydration, and healthy diet support lymphatic flow.
