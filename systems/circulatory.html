Circulatory System

The heart, blood, and vessels that deliver oxygen and nutrients to every cell.

Overview

The circulatory system moves blood throughout the body, transporting oxygen, nutrients, hormones, and waste. It includes the heart, blood vessels (arteries, veins, capillaries), and blood.

Main Functions

Transport: Delivers oxygen, nutrients, and hormones. Removal: Carries carbon dioxide and waste to lungs and kidneys. Protection: White blood cells fight infection. Regulation: Distributes heat and maintains pH balance.

Key Parts

Heart (4 chambers), arteries, veins, capillaries, and blood (plasma, red cells, white cells, platelets). Pulmonary circulation goes to the lungs; systemic circulation goes to the body.

Common Issues

Hypertension, atherosclerosis, heart attack, stroke, arrhythmia, and anemia. Exercise, diet, and not smoking protect cardiovascular health.
