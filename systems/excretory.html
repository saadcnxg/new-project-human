Excretory System

Removing waste products to keep the body's internal environment balanced.

Overview

The excretory system eliminates metabolic waste through the kidneys, bladder, lungs, skin, and liver. It maintains fluid, electrolyte, and acid-base balance.

Main Functions

Filtration: Kidneys filter blood. Reabsorption: Useful substances return to blood. Secretion: Waste moves into urine. Water balance: Regulates hydration and electrolytes.

Key Parts

Kidneys, ureters, bladder, urethra, lungs, skin, and liver (processing waste).

Common Issues

Kidney stones, UTIs, kidney failure, and dehydration. Drinking water and limiting salt and processed foods protect kidney health.
