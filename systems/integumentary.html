Integumentary System

Skin, hair, and nails — your body's largest and most visible organ system.

Overview

The integumentary system forms the body's outer covering. It includes the skin, hair, nails, and glands. The skin is the largest organ, weighing about 4 kg in adults.

Main Functions

Protection: Barrier against pathogens and injury. Temperature regulation: Sweating and blood flow. Sensation: Touch, pressure, pain, temperature. Vitamin D synthesis: Sunlight converts precursors in skin. Excretion: Sweat removes some waste.

Key Parts

Epidermis, dermis, hypodermis, hair follicles, nails, sebaceous glands, and sweat glands.

Common Issues

Acne, eczema, psoriasis, burns, skin cancer, and fungal infections. Sun protection and hygiene maintain skin health.
