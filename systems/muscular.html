Muscular System

Over 600 muscles that move your body, maintain posture, and generate heat.

Overview

The muscular system includes skeletal, smooth, and cardiac muscle. Skeletal muscles attach to bones via tendons and produce voluntary movement. Smooth muscles line organs, and cardiac muscle powers the heart.

Main Functions

Movement: Skeletal muscles pull on bones to move the body. Posture: Continuous muscle tone keeps you upright. Heat production: Shivering generates warmth. Protection: Muscles shield internal organs.

Key Parts

Major muscles include the deltoid (shoulder), pectoralis (chest), biceps, triceps, abdominals, quadriceps, hamstrings, gluteus maximus, and gastrocnemius (calf).

Common Issues

Strains, cramps, tendinitis, muscular dystrophy, and atrophy. Regular exercise and proper warm-ups keep muscles healthy.
