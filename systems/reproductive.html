Reproductive System

The organs that produce gametes and enable the creation of new life.

Overview

The reproductive system produces sex cells (gametes), hormones, and enables fertilization. Male and female systems differ in structure but share the goal of reproduction.

Main Functions

Gamete production: Eggs and sperm. Hormone production: Testosterone, estrogen, progesterone. Fertilization: Union of gametes. Development: Gestation and birth.

Key Parts

Male: testes, epididymis, vas deferens, prostate, penis. Female: ovaries, fallopian tubes, uterus, cervix, vagina.

Common Issues

Infertility, PCOS, endometriosis, STIs, and prostate issues. Regular screening and safe practices support reproductive health.
