Digestive System

Breaking down food into the nutrients that power your body.

Overview

The digestive system processes food through mechanical and chemical digestion, absorbs nutrients, and eliminates waste. It spans from the mouth to the anus.

Main Functions

Ingestion: Taking in food. Digestion: Breaking down food mechanically and chemically. Absorption: Nutrients enter the bloodstream. Elimination: Removing undigested waste.

Key Parts

Mouth, esophagus, stomach, small intestine, large intestine, liver, gallbladder, and pancreas.

Common Issues

Acid reflux, ulcers, IBS, Crohn's disease, constipation, and diarrhea. Fiber, hydration, and balanced diet support digestive health.
