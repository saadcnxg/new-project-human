Eye Health

Protect your vision with good habits and regular checkups.

Overview

Eye health involves preventing strain, protecting from UV damage, and catching problems early. Many eye diseases are silent until vision is affected.

Everyday Tips

Follow the 20-20-20 rule (look 20 ft away for 20 s every 20 min), blink often, keep screens at arm's length, use UV-protective sunglasses, and eat eye-friendly foods (leafy greens, carrots, fish).

Warning Signs

Sudden vision loss, eye pain, flashes, floaters, or red eyes — seek care immediately. Regular exams catch glaucoma, diabetic retinopathy, and macular degeneration early.
