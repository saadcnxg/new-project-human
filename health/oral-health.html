Oral Health

Your mouth is a gateway to overall health — protect it daily.

Overview

Oral health includes teeth, gums, and the entire mouth. Poor oral hygiene is linked to heart disease, diabetes, and respiratory infections.

Daily Routine

Brush twice daily with fluoride toothpaste for 2 minutes, floss daily, limit sugary snacks, drink water after meals, and avoid tobacco. Replace your toothbrush every 3–4 months.

Common Problems

Cavities, gum disease (gingivitis, periodontitis), bad breath, and enamel erosion. Regular dental checkups catch issues early.
