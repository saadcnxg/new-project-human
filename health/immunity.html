Immunity

How to strengthen your body's natural defense system.

Overview

Immunity is the body's ability to resist and fight infections. It involves barriers (skin, mucus), innate defenses (macrophages, inflammation), and adaptive defenses (T and B cells, antibodies).

Boosting Immunity

Sleep: Deep sleep supports T-cell function. Diet: Vitamin C, D, zinc, and probiotics support immunity. Exercise: Moderate activity reduces infection risk. Stress: Chronic stress weakens immunity — manage it. Hygiene: Wash hands, avoid touching face. Vaccines: Safe, effective way to train the immune system.

Myths

No single "superfood" or supplement can guarantee immunity. Focus on consistent healthy habits rather than quick fixes.
