Hydration

Water keeps every system working — from cells to joints to your brain.

Overview

The human body is about 60% water. Water regulates temperature, transports nutrients, lubricates joints, flushes waste, and supports every chemical reaction in the body.

Benefits

Improved energy and focus, healthier skin, better digestion, fewer headaches, and improved kidney function. Dehydration can cause dizziness, confusion, and constipation.

Tips to Stay Hydrated

Drink a glass of water upon waking, carry a reusable bottle, drink before and after exercise, and eat water-rich foods (cucumber, watermelon, oranges). Increase intake in hot weather and during illness.
