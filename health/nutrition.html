Nutrition

Fuel your body with the right balance of macronutrients, micronutrients, and water.

Overview

Nutrition is the science of how food nourishes the body. A balanced diet supplies energy, supports growth and repair, strengthens immunity, and lowers the risk of chronic disease.

Macronutrients

Carbohydrates: Main energy source — choose whole grains, fruits, and vegetables. Proteins: Build and repair tissues — lean meats, beans, dairy, and legumes. Fats: Support hormones and cell membranes — prefer unsaturated fats (olive oil, nuts, fish).

Micronutrients

Vitamins (A, B-complex, C, D, E, K) and minerals (calcium, iron, zinc, magnesium, potassium) play essential roles in metabolism, immunity, and bone health.

Healthy Eating Principles

Eat a variety of whole foods, limit processed foods, added sugars, and excess salt. Watch portion sizes, stay hydrated, and avoid skipping meals.
