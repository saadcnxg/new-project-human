Healthy Diet

What you eat shapes how you feel, think, and age.

Overview

A healthy diet emphasizes whole foods, variety, and balance. It reduces risk of heart disease, diabetes, and some cancers while improving energy and mood.

5+Fruit/veg servings
25-30 gFiber/day
<5 gSalt/day
<25 gAdded sugar/day

Daily Building Blocks

Vegetables and fruits: Half your plate. Whole grains: Oats, brown rice, whole wheat. Proteins: Fish, poultry, beans, eggs, nuts. Healthy fats: Olive oil, avocado, nuts. Dairy or alternatives: Calcium sources.

Limit or Avoid

Ultra-processed foods, sugary drinks, refined carbs, trans fats, and excessive salt. Cook more at home to control ingredients.
