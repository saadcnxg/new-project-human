Stress Management

Turn down the pressure and protect your body from chronic stress.

Overview

Stress is the body's response to challenges. Acute stress is useful; chronic stress raises cortisol and increases risk of heart disease, anxiety, and weakened immunity.

Effective Techniques

Deep breathing: Slow, diaphragmatic breaths activate the parasympathetic system. Meditation: 10 min daily reduces anxiety. Exercise: Burns off stress hormones. Time in nature: Lowers cortisol. Journaling and social support: Process emotions.

Reduce Stressors

Set boundaries, delegate tasks, limit news/social media, and practice saying no. If stress feels unmanageable, seek professional support.
