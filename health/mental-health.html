Mental Health

Your emotional, psychological, and social wellbeing — just as important as physical health.

Overview

Mental health affects how you think, feel, and act. It influences stress management, relationships, and decision-making. One in four people will experience a mental health issue at some point.

Pillars of Mental Health

Connection: Strong relationships with family and friends. Purpose: Meaningful work, hobbies, and goals. Movement: Regular exercise reduces anxiety and depression. Rest: Quality sleep is foundational. Mindfulness: Present-moment awareness reduces stress.

When to Seek Help

Persistent sadness, hopelessness, anxiety, mood swings, sleep changes, or thoughts of self-harm. Talk to a doctor, therapist, or call a mental health helpline.

Support Strategies

Practice self-care, set boundaries, limit social media, journal, and talk openly with trusted people. Professional help — therapy, counseling, or medication — is a sign of strength, not weakness.
