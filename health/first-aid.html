First Aid

Life-saving skills every person should know for emergencies.

Overview

First aid is the immediate care given to someone injured or suddenly ill until professional help arrives. Knowing basic skills can save lives and prevent complications.

CPR Basics

Check responsiveness, call emergency services, and begin chest compressions at 100–120 per minute, 5–6 cm deep, in the center of the chest. Use an AED if available.

Common Emergencies

Choking: Heimlich maneuver (abdominal thrusts). Bleeding: Apply direct pressure. Burns: Cool under running water for 20 min. Fainting: Lay flat, elevate legs. Seizure: Protect head, don't restrain. Anaphylaxis: Use epinephrine auto-injector.

Be Prepared

Keep a first-aid kit at home and in the car. Take a certified first-aid/CPR course. Know the emergency numbers for your region.
