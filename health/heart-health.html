Heart Health

Daily habits that keep your most vital muscle strong for decades.

Overview

Cardiovascular disease is the leading cause of death globally, yet up to 80% is preventable through lifestyle changes. Heart health is built on diet, movement, sleep, and stress management.

The Big Four

Diet: Mediterranean-style — fruits, vegetables, whole grains, fish, nuts, olive oil. Exercise: 150 min moderate cardio per week. Sleep: 7–9 hours. Stress: Manage with relaxation and social support.

Know Your Numbers

Check blood pressure, cholesterol, blood sugar, and BMI regularly. Family history matters, so share it with your doctor.
