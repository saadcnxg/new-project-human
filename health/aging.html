Healthy Aging

Aging is inevitable — declining health is not. Stay vibrant with the right habits.

Overview

Aging brings changes in muscle mass, bone density, cognition, and metabolism. Yet lifestyle choices strongly influence how we age and how long we stay independent.

~5%Muscle loss/decade after 30
150 minExercise/week
StrengthKey for independence
SocialBonds extend life

Key Habits

Stay active (cardio + strength + balance), eat nutrient-dense foods, sleep well, stay socially connected, keep learning, and manage chronic conditions. Get regular screenings.

Mind & Body

Challenge your brain with puzzles, reading, and new skills. Reduce fall risk with balance training. Protect skin from sun and stay hydrated.
