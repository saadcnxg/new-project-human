Weight Management

Achieving and maintaining a healthy weight through balanced habits, not crash diets.

Overview

Healthy weight is achieved when calorie intake matches energy expenditure. It's linked to better heart health, joint health, blood sugar control, and mental wellbeing.

Key Principles

Energy balance: Burn what you eat. Whole foods: Prefer unprocessed foods. Portion control: Use smaller plates and mindful eating. Regular activity: Combine cardio and strength training. Sleep: Poor sleep disrupts appetite hormones.

Avoiding Pitfalls

Skip crash diets, very low-calorie plans, and extreme restrictions. Focus on sustainable habits — slow, steady changes are easier to maintain. Consult a dietitian for personalized plans.
