Pregnancy

A 9-month journey of extraordinary change — nourish it well.

Overview

Pregnancy lasts about 40 weeks from the last menstrual period. It's divided into three trimesters, each with distinct changes and needs. Prenatal care is essential.

40 wkFull term
3Trimesters
400 μgFolic acid/day
8-10Prenatal visits

Nutrition

Folic acid prevents neural tube defects. Iron supports increased blood volume. Calcium and vitamin D support bone development. Avoid alcohol, raw fish, unpasteurized dairy, and excessive caffeine.

Healthy Habits

Attend all prenatal visits, stay active with doctor-approved exercise, sleep well, manage stress, and take recommended supplements. Watch for warning signs like severe headaches, bleeding, or reduced fetal movement.
