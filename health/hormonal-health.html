Hormonal Health

Balance your body's chemical messengers for energy, mood, and metabolism.

Overview

Hormones regulate metabolism, growth, reproduction, mood, and stress. Imbalances can cause fatigue, weight changes, mood swings, and irregular cycles.

Supporting Balance

Prioritize sleep, exercise regularly, eat a balanced diet with healthy fats and protein, manage stress, and limit alcohol and endocrine-disrupting chemicals (BPA, certain pesticides).

Warning Signs

Unexplained weight changes, fatigue, mood swings, irregular periods, hair loss, or temperature intolerance — see a doctor for hormone testing.
