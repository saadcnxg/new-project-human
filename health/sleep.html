Sleep

The nightly reset your brain and body need to repair, remember, and recharge.

Overview

Sleep is an active biological process essential for memory consolidation, hormone regulation, immune function, and tissue repair. Adults need 7–9 hours per night; teens need 8–10.

Stages of Sleep

Non-REM stages (light to deep sleep) restore the body and consolidate memory. REM sleep supports emotional processing and creativity. A full cycle lasts about 90 minutes.

Benefits

Better memory, mood, immune function, metabolism, and cardiovascular health. Chronic sleep deprivation increases risk of obesity, diabetes, and depression.

Sleep Hygiene Tips

Keep a consistent schedule, avoid screens 1 hour before bed, limit caffeine after noon, keep the room cool and dark, and exercise regularly (but not too close to bedtime).
