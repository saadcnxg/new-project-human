Exercise Types

Four pillars of movement — cardio, strength, flexibility, and balance.

Overview

A well-rounded fitness routine includes aerobic exercise, strength training, flexibility work, and balance practice. Each type delivers different benefits.

4Exercise types

The Four Types

Aerobic: Brisk walking, running, cycling, swimming — strengthens heart and lungs. Strength: Weights, resistance bands, bodyweight — builds muscle and bone. Flexibility: Stretching, yoga, Pilates — improves range of motion. Balance: Tai chi, single-leg stands — reduces fall risk.

Putting It Together

Aim for 150 min moderate cardio per week, 2+ strength sessions, and daily movement breaks. Warm up, cool down, and progress gradually to avoid injury.
