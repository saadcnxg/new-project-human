Lung Health

Breathe easier with habits that protect your lungs for life.

Overview

Lungs have remarkable capacity to heal, but they're vulnerable to smoke, pollution, and infections. Protecting lung function supports energy, exercise capacity, and longevity.

0Tobacco
PM2.5Pollution to avoid
ExerciseImproves capacity

Key Strategies

Avoid smoking and secondhand smoke, check air quality, exercise regularly to strengthen breathing muscles, get vaccinated, and treat allergies and asthma properly.

Warning Signs

Persistent cough, breathlessness, wheezing, or coughing blood should prompt medical evaluation. Lung cancer screening is recommended for long-term smokers.
