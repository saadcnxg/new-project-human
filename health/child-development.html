Child Development

Milestones in growth, movement, language, and learning from birth to teens.

Overview

Child development unfolds in stages — infancy, toddlerhood, childhood, and adolescence. Each stage builds physical, cognitive, social, and emotional skills.

0-18Years to adult
~1 kgBirth to 1 yr weight x3
~2 yrVocabulary explosion
~90%Brain growth by age 5

Key Domains

Physical: Motor skills from rolling to running. Cognitive: Thinking, problem-solving, memory. Language: From babbling to sentences. Social-emotional: Attachment, empathy, and self-regulation.

Supporting Development

Provide nutrition, sleep, play, reading, and warm responsive caregiving. Regular checkups track growth and milestones. Early intervention helps when concerns arise.
