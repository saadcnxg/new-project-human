Fitness

Regular movement that strengthens your heart, muscles, and mind.

Overview

Physical fitness combines cardiovascular endurance, muscular strength, flexibility, and balance. Regular exercise reduces risk of heart disease, diabetes, and depression while improving energy and sleep.

150 minCardio/week
2+ daysStrength/week
10KSteps ideal/day

Types of Exercise

Aerobic: Walking, running, cycling, swimming. Strength: Weight training, bodyweight exercises. Flexibility: Stretching, yoga, Pilates. Balance: Tai chi, single-leg stands.

Benefits

Stronger heart and lungs, better bone density, improved mood and cognition, weight control, and lower risk of chronic disease. Exercise also boosts immune function and longevity.

Getting Started

Start slowly, choose activities you enjoy, warm up and cool down, stay hydrated, and set realistic goals. Even short 10-minute sessions add up.
