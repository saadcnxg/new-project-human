Skin Care

Protect your body's largest organ with simple daily habits.

Overview

Skin care includes cleansing, moisturizing, sun protection, and avoiding harmful habits. Healthy skin supports immunity, sensation, and temperature regulation.

Core Routine

Cleanse: Gentle cleanser twice daily. Moisturize: Lock in hydration. Protect: Sunscreen every morning. Exfoliate: 1–2x per week. Sleep: Skin repairs at night. Hydrate: Drink water throughout the day.

Common Mistakes

Overwashing, skipping sunscreen on cloudy days, using too many products, and picking at blemishes. Avoid smoking and excessive alcohol — both accelerate skin aging.
