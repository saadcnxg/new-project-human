Digestive Health

A happy gut supports immunity, mood, and nutrient absorption.

Overview

The gut houses trillions of microbes that influence digestion, immunity, and even mental health. Digestive health is built on fiber, hydration, and a diverse diet.

Key Habits

Eat fiber-rich foods (fruits, vegetables, whole grains, legumes), include fermented foods (yogurt, kimchi, sauerkraut), drink water, chew slowly, and avoid excessive ultra-processed foods and alcohol.

Warning Signs

Persistent bloating, gas, changes in bowel habits, blood in stool, or unexplained weight loss — see a doctor. Screenings for colon cancer start at age 45.
