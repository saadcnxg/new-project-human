Vaccines

Train your immune system safely — one of medicine's greatest achievements.

Overview

Vaccines expose the immune system to harmless versions of pathogens, enabling it to recognize and fight them if exposed later. They prevent millions of deaths each year.

How They Work

Vaccines use inactivated pathogens, weakened live pathogens, or fragments (proteins, mRNA). The immune system produces antibodies and memory cells without causing disease.

Common Vaccines

MMR (measles, mumps, rubella), DTP (diphtheria, tetanus, pertussis), polio, hepatitis B, HPV, influenza, COVID-19, and pneumococcal. Childhood schedules vary by country — follow local guidance.

Safety & Side Effects

Mild side effects (soreness, low fever) are common and short-lived. Severe reactions are extremely rare. Vaccines are tested through rigorous clinical trials.
