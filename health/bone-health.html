Bone Health

Keep your skeleton strong for life with the right nutrition and movement.

Overview

Bones are living tissue that constantly rebuilds. Peak bone mass is reached around age 30, then gradually declines. Lifestyle choices determine how strong your bones stay.

Key Nutrients

Calcium: Dairy, leafy greens, fortified foods. Vitamin D: Sunlight, fatty fish, fortified milk, supplements. Protein: Supports bone matrix. Magnesium and vitamin K: Support bone structure.

Exercise

Weight-bearing exercises (walking, jogging, dancing) and resistance training stimulate bone remodeling. Balance exercises reduce fall risk.
