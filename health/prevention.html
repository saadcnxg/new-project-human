Prevention

Proactive habits that stop disease before it starts.

Overview

Prevention includes vaccination, screening, hygiene, and lifestyle choices. Many chronic diseases and infections are largely preventable with the right habits.

~40%Cancer preventable
~80%Heart disease preventable
~70%Type 2 diabetes preventable
1Health checkup/year

Key Strategies

Vaccination: Protects against flu, COVID-19, HPV, hepatitis, and more. Screening: Regular checks for blood pressure, cholesterol, blood sugar, and cancer (mammograms, colonoscopy). Hygiene: Handwashing, safe food handling, and oral care. Lifestyle: Don't smoke, limit alcohol, eat well, and exercise.

Mental & Social Prevention

Manage stress, maintain friendships, get enough sleep, and seek help early for mental health concerns.
